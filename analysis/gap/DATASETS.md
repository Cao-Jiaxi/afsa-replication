# The four `broad` datasets from WRDS

Generated by `analysis/gap/datasets.py` (design: CLAUDE.md §7, 2026-11-15). One setting on all four: training cost 5 bp, ridge 1e-4, 30 epochs, missing characteristics at rank 0, seeds 0–2; test years 1998–2021 and 2022–2025 with 8-year windows, validation 1996–1997 with 6-year windows. Every result is scored at the paper's costs (5 bp per unit of turnover, 1 bp per unit short), on stock-by-stock excess returns.

| Dataset | List | Entry rule | Training data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | plain: each month's 500 largest (the paper's universe) | 30-day wait: a signal from the 31st consecutive day on the list | `wrds_broad_fill-none` (day × slot arrays) |
| B | plain | rebuilt history: lags recomputed over the day's list; a signal once listed 30 days with accounts entered | `history\wrds_broad_fill-none` (month blocks) |
| C | buffered: the 500 largest plus last month's members still ranked within 600 | 30-day wait | `wrds_broad_buffer600_fill-none` |
| D | buffered | rebuilt history | `history\wrds_broad_buffer600_fill-none` |

A comparison, not a selection (CLAUDE.md §8): Table 2 stays on the plain list with rebuilt history (B), and the buffered list stays a robustness variant. The buffered datasets trade a larger list (about 545 securities a day), and the flat 5 bp does not charge more for the securities ranked 501–600.
