# Hyperparameter scan: the best net Sharpe ratio under each selection rule

Generated by `analysis/gap/selection.py` from the runs of `analysis/gap/scan.py`; read with `analysis/gap/REPORT.md`, section 10. Universe `broad`, seeds 0–2, every result scored at the paper's costs (5 bp per unit of turnover, 1 bp per unit short), whatever cost the model was trained with. Sharpe ratios are annualized, on stock-by-stock excess returns. A setting is *training cost, ridge, epochs* (and a band width for the last object).

- **Objects.** *model*: each seed's portfolio, statistics averaged over seeds (the paper's object). *ensemble*: the seeds' weights averaged and rescaled. *ensemble + band*: the ensemble through a no-trade band. The last two are deployment layers, never Table 2 rows.

- **Rules.** *validation*: best on 1996–1997, the paper's rule and CLAUDE.md §8. *walk-forward*: each year the best over the test years before it (1998 takes the validation choice); changes of setting are traded and paid for. *hindsight*: the best fixed setting over 1998–2021, a diagnostic upper bound. 2022–2025 is out of sample for all three.

- **Differences** are paired block bootstraps (21-day blocks, 2,000 draws) of pooled series against the baseline model pooled. Grid: 24 settings; with the band, 216 candidates per K.
