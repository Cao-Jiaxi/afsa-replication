# Look-back rules for walk-forward

Generated by `analysis/gap/lookback.py`. The design is CLAUDE.md §7 (the row of 2026-11-15, written before it ran); the reading is REPORT.md §10.8. Object: the seed ensemble (seeds 0–2) through a no-trade band, a deployment layer, never a Table 2 row. A candidate is a K, a training setting and a band width; its record is its daily net return traded alone from cash on 1998-01-02. Every result is scored at the paper's costs (5 bp per unit of turnover, 1 bp per unit short); Sharpe ratios are annualized, on stock-by-stock excess returns.

- **Rules.** Each January a rule scores every candidate on its record before that year and trades the best. 1998 takes the validation choice, ties go to the earlier candidate, and the book is carried over, so a switch is traded and paid for. *validation*: the best on 1996–1997, kept (the paper's rule). *all years*: the best net Sharpe ratio since 1998 (the current walk-forward). *last 8 years*: the best over the 8 most recent test years. *fading memory*: the best weighted net Sharpe ratio since 1998, each year weighted 0.5^(age/8), last year's age 0. *recent return, full risk*: the best mean net return over the last 8 years divided by the standard deviation since 1998. *hindsight*: the best over 1998–2021, kept (a diagnostic). The two rules with an 8-year window equal *all years* until 2006.

- **Decision, fixed before the run.** A rule beats *all years* only if its 1998–2021 net Sharpe ratio is higher at K = 8, at K = 30 and with K chosen; no single year of 1998–2021, traded as *all years* instead, reverses any of the three; and its 2022–2025 net Sharpe ratio is not lower in any of the three. A difference is real only if its paired bootstrap interval (21-day blocks, 2,000 draws) excludes 0.

- **Check.** The *validation*, *all years* and *hindsight* plans equal `selection.py`'s (asserted), so those rows reproduce `SCAN_BUFFER_GRID.md` and, in the second check, `SCAN.md`.
