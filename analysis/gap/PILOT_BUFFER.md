# Pilot on the validation years: where the scan's levers matter

Generated by `analysis/gap/pilot.py`; read with `analysis/gap/REPORT.md`, section 10.6. Every model is trained on 6-year windows and scored on 1996–1997 only (`wrds_broad_buffer600_fill-none`, entry rule `history`, seeds 0, 1, 2); nothing after 1997 is read. Scored at the paper's costs (5 bp per unit of turnover, 1 bp per unit short), whatever cost the model was trained with. Sharpe ratios are annualized, on stock-by-stock excess returns.

- **Settings** are *training cost, ridge, epochs*. The model is each seed's portfolio, statistics averaged over seeds (the paper's object). The layer is the seeds' ensemble through a no-trade band, its width the best of 9 on the same two years; a deployment layer, never a Table 2 row.

- **Differences** are paired block bootstraps (21-day blocks, 2,000 draws) of pooled series: the seeds' average daily net return for the model, the ensemble's own series for the layer. *Clearly worse* (†): the 95% interval of the difference from the best lies below 0. The best is the maximum of many noisy figures, so † appears a little too often: use it to drop ranges, not points.

- **Reference:** 5 bp, 1e-4, 30 ep, the setting of the rebuilt-history runs.
