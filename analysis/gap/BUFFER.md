# The buffered list against the plain one

Generated by `analysis/gap/buffer.py`; the list is described in `NOTES.md` §8. One setting on both lists: training cost 5 bp, ridge 1e-4, 30 epochs, the rebuilt-history entry rule (`--entry_rule history`), missing characteristics at rank 0, seeds 0–2. Every result is scored at the paper's costs (5 bp per unit of turnover, 1 bp per unit short), on stock-by-stock excess returns.

- **Plain list** (`wrds_broad`): each month's 500 largest securities, the paper's universe (the runs of SCAN_HISTORY.md).

- **Buffered list** (`wrds_broad_buffer600`): each month's 500 largest plus the securities of last month's list that still rank within 600 (500 to 564 a month). It contains the plain list. A robustness variant, not a Table 2 row: it departs from the paper's 500 largest, and the flat 5 bp cost does not charge more for the smaller securities ranked 501–600.
